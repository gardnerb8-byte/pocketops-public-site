Privacy Policy

PocketOps is an AI admin assistant for solo service businesses. This page describes the types of information the app may collect and how that information is used to provide app features.

Information PocketOps may collect

  • Account information such as email address, user ID, authentication metadata, and sign-in status.
  • Business profile data such as business name, owner name, service type, phone number, and default hourly rate.
  • Client and contact details entered by the user, including names, email addresses, phone numbers, addresses, and notes.
  • Jobs, invoices, reminders, payment status, reminder dates, and related notes entered by the user.
  • AI prompt inputs and generated quote/customer messages, including client name, job details, estimated price, business name, and tone.
  • AI usage events such as generation timestamps, source, model, and estimated input/output character counts.
  • Subscription and purchase status metadata processed through RevenueCat, such as entitlement status, product IDs, store environment, and purchase identifiers.
  • Local notification permission state and device-local notification IDs for reminder notifications on the current device.

Service providers

PocketOps may use the following providers to operate the app:

  • Supabase for authentication, database storage, and Edge Functions.
  • OpenAI for AI-generated quote/customer message drafts through server-side Supabase Edge Functions.
  • RevenueCat for subscription status, purchase handling, entitlement data, and subscription webhook processing.
  • Expo and EAS for mobile app development and build tooling.

How information is used

PocketOps uses information to provide app features, save user-entered business records, generate AI quote drafts, show AI usage summaries, manage subscription status, schedule local reminders, troubleshoot app behavior, and support account deletion requests.

Account deletion

Users can request account deletion in the app by going to Settings, then Delete Account. In Supabase-backed mode, PocketOps calls a secure server-side delete-account function to remove user-owned app records and delete the Supabase auth user. In demo mode, no real account is created; deletion clears local demo data on the device.

Deleted user-owned records may include business profile data, clients, jobs, invoices, reminders, AI messages, AI usage records, AI limit records, subscription entitlement rows, and subscription refresh rows where appropriate. Operational records such as RevenueCat webhook logs may be preserved for security, debugging, fraud prevention, or subscription reconciliation purposes where allowed.

Data security

PocketOps is designed so server-only secrets, such as OpenAI API keys, Supabase service role keys, RevenueCat secret API keys, and webhook tokens, are stored in server-side configuration and not in the mobile app.

Your responsibilities

Users are responsible for the client/contact data and business records they enter into PocketOps, including making sure their use complies with applicable laws and obligations to customers.
