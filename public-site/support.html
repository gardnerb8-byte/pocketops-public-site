Common support topics

Account deletion

Go to Settings, then Delete Account, then type DELETE to confirm. If that flow is unavailable, contact support for help.

Billing and subscriptions

PocketOps uses RevenueCat for future Pro subscription support. Store purchase and restore behavior depends on App Store or Google Play configuration.

AI quote generation

AI-generated quote messages are drafts. Review and edit generated text before sending it to a customer.

Local reminders

Reminder notifications are local to the current device and require notification permission on iOS or Android.
