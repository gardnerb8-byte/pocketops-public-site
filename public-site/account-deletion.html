Account Deletion

PocketOps includes an in-app account deletion flow. Users can delete a demo account locally or request deletion for a Supabase-backed account through the secure server-side account deletion function.

How to delete your account in the app

  1. Open PocketOps.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the deletion explanation.
  5. Type DELETE to confirm.
  6. Tap Delete account.

Demo mode deletion

In demo mode, no real PocketOps account is created. Deleting a demo account clears local demo data on the current device where practical and signs the user out.

Supabase-backed deletion

In Supabase-backed mode, PocketOps calls a secure server-side delete-account Edge Function. The mobile app does not store or use the Supabase service role key. The function is designed to delete user-owned PocketOps records and the Supabase auth user.

User-owned data may include:

  • Business profile data.
  • Clients and contact records entered by the user.
  • Jobs, invoices, and reminders.
  • AI message history.
  • AI usage events and AI limit records.
  • Subscription entitlement rows owned by the user.
  • Subscription refresh events owned by the user.

Operational records

PocketOps may preserve limited operational records where appropriate, such as RevenueCat webhook logs, backend sync logs, security records, fraud-prevention records, or records needed for subscription reconciliation. These records are not exposed to normal app users.

Support request fallback

If in-app deletion is unavailable, users can request help through support.
